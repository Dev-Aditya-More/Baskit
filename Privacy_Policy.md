# Privacy Policy for Baskit

Last Updated: May 2026

## Introduction

Baskit is a food product scanning and nutrition insights application designed to help users make healthier food choices.

Your privacy is important. This Privacy Policy explains what information Baskit collects, how it is used, and how your data is handled.

By using Baskit, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

---

# Information We Collect

## 1. Barcode Scan Data

Baskit may process barcode data entered or scanned by users to fetch product information and nutrition details.

Scanned barcode data is used only for providing app functionality.

---

## 2. Product Search Queries

When manually searching products, search terms may be processed to retrieve matching food products and nutrition information.

---

## 3. Local App Data

Baskit may store the following locally on your device:

- Scan history
- Health insights
- Product lookup history
- User preferences
- Cached nutrition data

This data remains on your device unless explicitly removed by the user.

---

# Camera Permission

Baskit requests camera access only for barcode scanning functionality.

The camera is not used in the background and no camera footage is stored or transmitted.

---

# Internet Usage

Baskit uses internet access to:

- Fetch food product information
- Retrieve nutrition details
- Access barcode databases
- Load related product metadata

---

# Third-Party Services

Baskit may use third-party services such as:

- OpenFoodFacts API
- Google ML Kit Barcode Scanning

These services may process limited technical information required for app functionality.

Please review their respective privacy policies for additional details.

---

# Data Sharing

Baskit does not sell personal data.

Baskit does not share personal information with third parties except when required for core app functionality or legal obligations.

---

# Data Security

Reasonable measures are taken to protect user data and maintain app security.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission over the internet is completely secure.

---

# Children's Privacy

Baskit is not directed toward children under the age of 13.

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

# Your Choices

Users may:

- Clear app data
- Remove scan history
- Uninstall the app at any time

---

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically.

Changes will be reflected by updating the "Last Updated" date above.
